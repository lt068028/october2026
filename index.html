Speaking Practice & Sentence Building

Practise Japanese and German by building sentences from prompts and saying your answers aloud. Selected exercises use your microphone and speech recognition, so you can practise speaking without typing every answer.

Can I practise speaking Japanese without typing?

Yes. Selected Japanese exercises let you answer aloud using your microphone. Practise forming sentences and responding to questions with speech recognition.

Practice 8.1: VerbのがAdj, Practice 8.2: 何か・何も・何でも, Practice 8.3: だれが？何が？

How can I practise building Japanese sentences?

Start with English prompts and form your own Japanese sentences. You can also practise expressing ideas and answering questions in Japanese.

English ➡️ Japanese Approach (いAdj), Practice 8.1: VerbのがAdj, Practice 8.2: 何か・何も・何でも, Practice 8.3: だれが？何が？

Can I practise speaking German?

Yes. Deutsche Übung 1.1 lets you build German sentences from cues and answer aloud using your microphone.
