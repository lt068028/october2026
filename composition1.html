⏹️Autostop ON: Automatically stops recording. OFF: Records until you click the stop button.
▶️Autoplay ON: Plays your voice automatically after recording. OFF: Disables automatic playback.
🔊Want to change the voice? Check⚙️
Win: Settings > Time & Language > Speech
Mac/iOS: Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content
ChromeOS: Settings > Advanced > Accessibility > Text-to-Speech
