⏹️Autostop Automatically stops recording when you stop speaking. ⏹️Manual stop Records continuously until you click the stop button.
▶️Autoplay Plays the model and your voice automatically after recording. ⏯️Manual play Disables automatic playback.
🔊Want to change the voice? Check⚙️
Win: Settings > Time & Language > Speech
Mac/iOS: Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content
ChromeOS: Settings > Advanced > Accessibility > Text-to-Speech
